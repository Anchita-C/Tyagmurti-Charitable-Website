Our Story

Our History

Our institution was established in 1985 with a single, powerful vision: to serve the poor and needy at extremely subsidised rates and to uplift society through collective participation.

The foundation of this trust was laid under the guidance of the revered (Poojniya) Shri Vishwanath Acharya Ji (11 November 1920 – 1976), a saintly soul whose entire life was devoted to selfless service. The trust was formally established by Sri Satsang Sabha, Suraj Ganj (Registered Mandir).

Our Founder

Born on 11/11/1920 in Himachal Pradesh, Shri Vishwanath Acharya Ji received his primary education there and later moved to Firozpur for higher studies. After completing his education, he travelled extensively across almost every part of India and abroad. These journeys deepened his understanding of society and strengthened his lifelong mission: serving humanity without expectation of financial gain.

For him, money was never a criterion. He believed that true service lies in purity of intent and dedication to the welfare of others. He was known for his pious nature, dedication, and simplicity, inspiring countless people to walk the path of service.

A Movement Built by the People

In its early days, the institution began with very small donations — sometimes as little as Re. 1. The philosophy was simple yet profound:

"Built by people, strengthened by participation, and rooted in the society it serves."

Over time, this belief bore fruit. While many contributed modestly, others donated generously — even to the tune of several lakhs — helping the institution grow while staying rooted in its original values.

Continuity of Leadership and Service

During the lifetime of Shri Vishwanath Acharya Ji, the responsibilities of the institution were entrusted to Punshi Shri Satnam Das Ji, who served diligently from 1996 to 2006. After his passing, the trust unanimously elected Dr. Vir Bhushan Acharya (MSE, MPhil, PhD) as Chairman of the Trust.

Dr. Acharya retired as Vice Principal and Head of the Post Graduate Department of Physics at DAV College, Jalandhar. He has also served as Principal and Director of educational institutions, bringing extensive experience in education, leadership, and administration.

Since assuming responsibility for the trust, Dr. Acharya has continued its mission with integrity, transparency, and compassion. He is strongly supported by Mrs. Kiran Acharya, who actively contributes to extending the trust's services for the welfare of society.

Guidance, Blessings, and Support

With the blessings and support of Shri Sheetal Vij Ji, Chief Editor, Dainik Savera and Patron of the Institution, the trust continues to progress steadily and meaningfully.

The trust remains proudly based in Jalandhar, Punjab, India and continues to uphold its founding ideals of selfless service, collective responsibility, and compassion for humanity.
