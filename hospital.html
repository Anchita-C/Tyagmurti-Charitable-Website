Departments

Dentistry

The Dentistry Department of our charitable institution is well-equipped to provide comprehensive oral healthcare at highly affordable costs. The department houses five modern dental chairs and offers a wide range of services including tooth removal, fillings, root canal treatments, and other routine dental procedures. Equipped with top-quality, advanced dental equipment, the department ensures safe, hygienic, and effective treatment for all patients. Our services are delivered by well-trained and experienced medical staff who are committed to providing compassionate care while maintaining the highest standards of dental practice.

Orthopedics

The Orthopaedic Department of the hospital provides reliable and affordable care for patients with bone and joint conditions. The department offers orthopaedic check-ups, fracture diagnosis, and treatment, along with evaluation and management of common musculoskeletal problems. Supported by appropriate diagnostic facilities and experienced medical staff, the department is committed to ensuring timely care, proper healing, and improved mobility for patients, in line with the institution's charitable mission.
